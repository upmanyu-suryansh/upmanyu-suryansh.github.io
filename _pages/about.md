Welcome to my webpage! I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Economics department at the University of Texas at Austin. My research interests lie in macroeconomics and labor economics.

In my research, I document the extent of mismatch between the skills firms want and the skills workers have, and its implications for labor market policy. I also study the effectiveness of pay transparency laws in providing wage information and improving labor market outcomes, using data on the near-universe of U.S. job postings.

**I am on the job market in the academic year 2026-2027.**
